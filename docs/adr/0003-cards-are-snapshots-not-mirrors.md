# Cards are snapshots, not live mirrors

When the Tutor condenses a Lesson part into Board Cards, each Card stores a
copy of the text at creation time plus a Citation to its source block. It
does not stay linked to the Lesson's current content. The alternative —
cards that mirror the Lesson — was rejected because it creates unsolvable
edit conflicts (user edits the Card while the Tutor rewrites the Lesson) for
a product where "the Card is yours the moment it exists" is the point.

**Consequence:** if the Tutor later rewrites a Lesson, existing Cards keep
the old text (intentional: notes are what you captured at the time). Card
text and Lesson text never sync.
